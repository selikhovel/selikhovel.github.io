Things I have designed and shipped, including work where the code is not
public. For a product engineer the interesting part is rarely the repository. It
is what the thing does, who it is for, and which calls were made along the way.

## What each entry should contain

Written for a reader deciding whether to hire, so lead with the product, not
the stack.

- **What it is** — one sentence a non-engineer understands.
- **The problem** — who had it, and what was wrong before.
- **What I decided** — the two or three judgement calls that mattered, and what
  was given up for them. This is the part that distinguishes a product engineer
  from an implementer, and it is the part that is almost always missing.
- **What shipped** — live link, or a screenshot when the product is private.
- **My role and the stack** — briefly, at the end.

Keep the code link optional. A closed repository with a working product beats
an open repository with none.
