This is a personal site. It sells nothing, runs no advertising, and does not
track anyone across the web. What follows is what happens when you open a page,
written plainly rather than in the usual boilerplate.

## Who is responsible

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft
company in the United States. Like any web server, it records requests, and
those records include the visitor's IP address, the page requested, the time,
and the browser's user agent. That processing is necessary to deliver the site
at all, and rests on legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub
describes its own handling in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Statistics

Visits are counted with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com). It was
chosen specifically because it sets no cookies, assigns no persistent
identifier, and does not retain personal data, which is why this site shows no
consent banner. What it records is which page was opened, the referring site,
and coarse browser and country information. GoatCounter documents its own
handling in its [privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

The download link for the CV is counted the same way, so that it is possible to
tell whether the document is taken rather than the pages read.

## Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies.

Your choice of light or dark theme is kept in your browser's local storage so
the site does not change appearance between visits. It never leaves your device
and is never sent anywhere.

## Fonts and other files

Everything the pages load comes from this domain, including the fonts. They are
downloaded when the site is built rather than fetched from Google when you open
a page, so no request goes to Google and your address is not disclosed to it.

The site framework emits one connection hint pointing at `cdnjs.cloudflare.com`,
a leftover from a feature this site does not use. No file is loaded from it, but
a browser may open a connection to it in anticipation.

## Writing to me

If you send an email, the message and your address are processed in order to
reply, and kept for as long as the exchange is useful. There is no contact form
on this site and no message is stored on the server.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to data about you, its correction or
erasure, restriction of processing, and you may object to processing based on
legitimate interest. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. For
Berlin that is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Most of what is described above never reaches me. The server logs belong to
GitHub, and the statistics are not linked to any individual, so a request about
your own data will usually be answered by pointing you at the two providers
named here.
